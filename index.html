RULES & GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY

ORIGINAL MODE Standard Gomoku
  • Objective: Be the first player to form an unbroken line of 5 stones horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • Match Format: Best of 3 rounds (First to 2 wins takes the series).
  • Turn Timer: 30 seconds per move. If time runs out, a stone is auto-placed randomly.
ITEM WAR MODE Obstacles & Hidden Bombs
  • 🌊🏔️ 5 Nature Obstacles (Lakes & Mountains): Placed randomly at match start. No stones can ever be placed on lakes or mountains!
  • 💣 2 Hidden Bombs: Inconspicuously hidden within 5 cells of the board center (7, 7). They look like normal empty spots until stepped on!
  • 💥 Explosion & Destruction: Placing a stone on a bomb detonates it! All stones in a 5-cell cross (center + up, down, left, right) are destroyed immediately!
  • 🌋 Permanent Ruins: The 5 blast cells turn into charred debris craters. Neither player can place stones on ruins for the rest of the game!
RANK BADGES Single Play Milestones

Reach Stage 3 (⭐), Stage 5 (⭐⭐), Stage 8 (⭐⭐⭐), and conquer Stage 10 for the legendary Diamond Master (💎) mark! Badges persist permanently.
